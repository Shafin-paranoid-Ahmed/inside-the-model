# Lesson & figure audit — 2026-10-05

Scope: every drafted lesson, Day 2 → Day 15 plus the math primer, after the owner's Day 1 review notes.
Day 1 was fixed in the same session (see "Day 1 — fixed" at the end).

Problem classes, from the owner's Day 1 notes:

- **R — repeated figure.** The same widget with the same settings appears twice or more in a lesson under a different caption. The reader sees the same animation again and has to work out what is new. (Day 1: residuals / MSE / knob / outlier graphs.)
- **M — figure doesn't show what the prose claims.** The step talks about something the figure does not visibly demonstrate. (Day 1: one-hot example had no effect on the plot.)
- **U — unclear or poorly written section.** (Day 1: classification loss.)
- **I — interaction bug.** The widget throws away what the reader did. (Day 1: LineFit replayed its default guess after the reader placed their own.)
- **T — runtime.** Console errors, main-thread stalls, horizontal overflow on mobile.

Severity: **high** = a reader is misled or stuck; **med** = confusing or wasteful; **low** = polish.

## Cross-cutting

- **Interaction: "Play discards what you did."** Seven widgets use `$effect(() => { if (playing) over = null })`: DotProduct, LogisticFit, LossPlayground, ModelKnobs, NeuronPlayground, PolyOverfit, SoftmaxBars. In LossPlayground, ModelKnobs, DotProduct, NeuronPlayground, PolyOverfit and SoftmaxBars, Play means "resume the demo", and the prose never asks the reader to play from their own setting, so it is acceptable. LogisticFit is a real bug (listed under Day 2). LineFit had the same bug and is fixed.
- **Timeline seconds in prose.** Five lessons quote timeline times ("at 3 s", "watch the first three seconds"): svm-kernels, neuron, convolution, channels-stride-padding, resnets. The `t = … s` readout shows timeline seconds, so the numbers still match it; only "watch the first three seconds" now takes six real seconds at the new 0.5× default. Low priority.
- **Root cause of the R findings.** Lessons written early (math primer, Days 1–2, Day 5) reuse one rich widget per lesson with only a title change. Lessons written later (Day 3, Days 6–11) give every figure a `mode`. The fix pattern is the same everywhere: a per-figure `view`/`mode` prop so each figure does one job, as done for Day 1.

Suggested rule for `LESSONS.md` / `BUILDING.md`: **no two figures in a lesson may have identical props apart from the title**, and a check in `scripts/` that fails the build when they do (the scan used for this audit is ~30 lines).

## Runtime (all 72 drafted lessons, desktop with every step visited + mobile, light and dark)

`scripts/check-page.mjs` on the production build: **no console errors and no horizontal overflow on any page, desktop or mobile.** Frame rate ~75 fps everywhere it was measured. Flagged pages were re-run one at a time to remove CPU contention from the parallel run:

- **T (high) train-val-test: 480–490 ms main-thread stalls on every step, both themes.** Cause: `makeRun()` (`src/components/widgets/DataSplitter/sim.ts:83`) trains a degree-14 polynomial for 1,000 epochs synchronously on the main thread, at hydration and on every split change (`DataSplitter.svelte:34`). Fix: precompute the default split's run at build time, or move `makeRun`/`makeTune` into a worker (the architecture rule already allows `worker.ts`), or train fewer epochs and interpolate.
- **T (low) k-nearest-neighbors: 160 ms / 107 ms (light / dark), step 4** (the `mode="k"` sweep, which fits every k on first view). Just over budget in both themes.
- **T (low) cold-start only, 107–173 ms on the first (light) load, fine on the warm (dark) load:** decision-trees, ensembles, svm-kernels, decision-boundaries, linear-regression, overfitting-bias-variance, regularization, neuron, optimizers. One-off hydration cost per page, just over the 100 ms budget; worth a look when doing performance work, not urgent.
- Contention noise only (clean when re-run alone): ppo, feature-visualization, logistic-regression.
